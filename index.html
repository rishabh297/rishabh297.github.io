Nº 00 Prologue

Hey, I'm Rishabh. A founder and applied-ML researcher, and a compulsive builder of things that, with any luck, make life a little smarter.

Right now I'm building Bond Health, an AI company I co-founded and run as CEO. We use large language models to read electronic health records and match patients to the clinical trials they actually qualify for, turning a months-long, multi-billion-dollar bottleneck into something that takes weeks. Alongside it, I'm finishing a Master's in Biomedical Informatics at Harvard Medical School.

Before Bond, I co-founded Doorstep.ai (last-mile delivery, computer vision), taught LLMs to read single-cell genomics at Regeneron, and built clinical ML at GEn1E Lifesciences that earned a $749K federal grant. Along the way I've published in NEJM AI, filed patents, and was named one of Boston's 25 Under 25.

But the part I actually care about is simpler: hard problems, fast feedback loops, and building things people genuinely use.

Bond Health ↗

Getting patients into the right trials.

The company I co-founded and run as CEO. Bond is an LLM-native platform that reads structured EHR signals and free-text clinical notes to match patients to the trials they actually qualify for, then carries them through to informed consent. It's grounded in a clinical knowledge graph of 1.6M+ nodes and backed by Plug and Play, Leo Capital, and CEAS Investments.

90%+trial-match accuracy vs. a ~67% public baseline

LLMs for single-cell biology.

On Regeneron's Molecular Profiling & Data Science team, I built a Python package that wires proprietary LLMs into the company's single-cell RNA-seq workflow, turning a prompt and a dataset into automated biological summaries.
